## What Fluxa is
Fluxa is an AI workflow automation platform for operations teams. It connects the tools a company already uses and runs AI agents that automate repetitive work: weekly reporting, approvals, data entry between systems, alerts when a metric changes, and first-line answers to internal questions.

## Who it is for
Operations, revenue operations and business teams at B2B companies with 20 to 2,000 employees. Typical buyers: Head of Operations, RevOps Manager, COO, Business Operations Lead.

## Main use cases
1. **Automated reporting:** builds the weekly business report from your tools and sends it with a short summary of what changed and why.
2. **Metric alerts:** watches key numbers (revenue, pipeline, tickets, stock) and alerts the owner with a likely cause when something moves.
3. **Approvals:** routes requests (discounts, purchases, access) to the right person, with context, and records the decision.
4. **Data sync:** keeps CRM, spreadsheets and project tools consistent without copy-paste.
5. **Internal assistant:** answers team questions from your documentation and data.

## How it works
- No-code workflow builder plus AI agents that can read, decide and act inside approved tools.
- Human-in-the-loop: any step can require approval before acting.
- Every action is logged and can be reviewed.

## Integrations (demo list)
Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, Jira, Airtable, PostgreSQL, and any REST API through webhooks.
